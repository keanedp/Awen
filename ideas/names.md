# Name ideas

Candidates considered when renaming the app from "Writer" (2026-09-26). **Chosen: Awen.**

Availability (app stores, trademarks, domains) hasn't been checked for any of these.

## Myths and gods

- **Awen** (Welsh/Druidic): poetic inspiration, the "flowing spirit" given to bards. *Chosen.*
- **Ogma** (Irish): god of eloquence, said to have invented the Ogham alphabet.
- **Imbas** (Irish): the illuminating inspiration of poets.
- **Brigid** (Celtic): goddess of poetry.
- **Kvasir** (Norse): the wisest being, whose blood became the mead of poetry.
- **Mímir** (Norse): keeper of the well of wisdom.
- **Calliope** (Greek): muse of epic poetry.
- **Mnemosyne** (Greek): memory, mother of the Muses. Hard to spell.

## Latin

- **Stilus**: the Roman stylus for wax tablets; root of "style".
- **Tabula**: writing tablet (*tabula rasa*).
- **Cera**: the wax on those tablets.
- **Calamus**: reed pen.
- **Scriba**: scribe.
- **Littera**: a letter of the alphabet.
- **Verba**: words.
- **Otium**: cultivated leisure, the quiet time for thinking and writing.
- **Nota**: a mark or note.
- **Palimpsest**: a page scraped clean and written over.

## Other European languages

- **Heft** (German): notebook, exercise book.
- **Feder** (German): feather, quill.
- **Stille** (German): stillness.
- **Quaderno** (Italian): notebook.
- **Cahier** (French): notebook.
- **Tinta** (Spanish/Portuguese): ink.
- **Kalamos** (Greek): reed pen.
- **Rún** (Old Norse/Irish): secret, whisper; root of "rune".
